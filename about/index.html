About SACSAB

Swedish Airspace Control Systems AB (SACSAB) is an engineering and defense technology company based in Uppsala, Sweden. We design reliable physical computing architectures and smart airborne tracking platforms to safeguard low-altitude airspace surrounding critical infrastructure.

Founded in 2026 by engineering students from Uppsala University, SACSAB combines specialized technical expertise across embedded hardware engineering, real-time signal processing, and autonomous control systems.

R&D & Platform Engineering

SACSAB actively iterates on proprietary counter-UAS flight hardware, telemetry interfaces, and multi-sensor sensor fusion pipelines.
