About SACSAB

Swedish Airspace Control Systems AB (SACSAB) is an airspace defense company based in Uppsala, Sweden. We design reliable physical computing architectures and smart airborne tracking platforms built to protect the airspace surrounding complex areas.

Established in 2026, SACSAB was founded by five civil engineers out of Uppsala University. Our team have different focuses covering embedded systems, computer systems and AI.

Current status

SACSAB is in an early start-up phase, focusing on early idea exploration and prototyping.
